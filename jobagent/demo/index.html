How it works

1) Connect portals
Log in to Naukri/LinkedIn in your browser. The extension reads the job form in real time.
2) Review & confirm
The agent fills fields, proposes answers, and highlights missing/low-confidence items.
3) Track outcomes
All applications are logged with timestamps, company, role, and ATS score for iteration.

Engineering note: handlers should sanitize regex skills (e.g., C++) and preserve line breaks in descriptions.

FAQ

Is this fully automated submission?

It is an assisted application tool. You review key fields and remain responsible for the content you submit.

Will portals block my account?

Risk depends on portal policies and usage patterns. The product should enforce pacing, retries, and avoid aggressive automation.

What does “Basic ATS scoring” mean?

A lightweight keyword/skills match score between the JD text and your CV/profile content. It is not a guarantee of selection.

Does WhatsApp notification work on Free plan?

No. WhatsApp notifications are offered on paid plans due to messaging infrastructure costs.
